DOCSTRING:
The orbit: people who are not the cast but belong to somebody in it.

Rena's little sister existed as one sentence in `cast_facts` ("swims on
Saturday mornings", three mentions) and nothing else -- no identity, so the
next week could make her a brother, and no standing, so nothing anybody
felt about her could be carried. This is the registry: one row per person,
owned by a cast member, with a relation, a name only if one was ever given,
the facts the record has put on them, what is going on with them now, and
two standings -- how Tomoe stands with them and how their owner does --
each a sentence and a slow number.

Code decides when (the compile, once a day) and keeps the rows honest
(match by owner+relation or by name; a name the roster owns is refused;
nudges clamp to +-2 and numbers to 0-100; caps); the `orbit` role decides
what was said about whom today. Readers: the exchange (a speaker knows the
people in their own life), the planner (a beat can be about somebody's
sister), her own prompt (the people around her friends), and the gossip
pool (an orbit person is off limits, like the cast). kv `orbit`; `/orbit`.
