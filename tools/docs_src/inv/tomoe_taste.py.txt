DOCSTRING:
Her taste against the account she inherited.

The X account was his: 305 followed accounts, 302 of them from before she
took it. She sees twenty-five posts a look, four looks a day, so her
reaction sheet (kv `x_taste`) only ever covers the thirty loudest accounts
and the other 270 are never judged; at one unfollow a day after three
dislikes of posts she may never see, the account would stay his for years.
His ask (2026-08-26): over time the account should become hers.

Two things here. `judge` reads every carded account against her declared
taste (`data/taste.json`, his to edit) once, in batches, and writes a
verdict per handle to kv `x_verdicts`: keep (hers), leave (not hers, not
offensive; his, tolerated), drop (she would unfollow). `Curation.drift`
then unfollows from the drop list at a paced rate, and the look session
runs one of her `queries` now and then so there are accounts of her own to
follow. `/x drift` is the ruler: inherited remaining, hers added, dropped.
