DOCSTRING:
How the first character comes to know what he does: the app on her phone.

Every phrase here is the wording the shared library carried before the words
moved out of it, byte for byte, so nothing she reads changed. Registered under
"app" in `sim/sight.py`; a pair is (English, Japanese) for the persona's `t()`.

The exception is what he has been watching, which was hers for the first time
on 2026-09-04 and is not a translation of anything the library used to say:
she was not there when he played it and the app told her afterwards, so those
phrases are written from that distance rather than from his shoulder.

`{ed}` in those phrases is which EDITION of a franchise he watched -- the OVA,
the film, a one-episode special -- and it is empty for an ordinary television
run. MyAnimeList gives two Example Show entries the identical Japanese title, so
a line built from the title and the episode number alone could not say which
of them he had opened.
