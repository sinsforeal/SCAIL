# tomoe/world\zeitgeist.py  (105 lines)

DOCSTRING:
What is going on in Japan right now, as it reaches her.

`tools/zeitgeist.py` fetches and filters; this reads the file it writes
(`world.zeitgeist_file`), reloading when the file changes, the way the
calendar picks up local events. Readers: her system prompt (what she has
seen on her phone or heard this week), the director (a scene can have it
on the TV or in the group chat), the beat writer (the group talks about
what everyone is talking about), the social read (her X reactions know the
week), the diary and her posts.

DEFS:

    _hook(it): Why it reaches her, or nothing when the reason names a railway she does not ride.  The check is at the fetch as well (`tools/zeitgeist.py`), which is where a bad reason should die. This is the second one, and it earns it
    brief(limit, today): One line per item: the Japanese as she met it, the gloss, and why it reaches her. Empty string when nothing is current.
