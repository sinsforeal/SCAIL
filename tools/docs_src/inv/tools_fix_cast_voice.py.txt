# tools\fix_cast_voice.py  (112 lines)

DOCSTRING:
Every line of the six that breaks their own sheet.

Found by reading all 173 turns of Yumi, Kenji, Kaito, Rena, Aya and Hinako,
then encoding what the read-through found as rules in `data/voice_rules.json`
and letting the checker produce the list mechanically -- so this is the full
set the spec knows about, not the subset I happened to notice.

What was wrong, by kind:

- **Yumi talking about herself in the third person.** 「スーパーの由美さんが言って
  たんだけど」-- she IS Yumi and she works at that supermarket. The model lost
  track of who was speaking and invented a colleague with her own name.
- **Yumi calling herself ママ**, where this house says お母さん (Kaito's ママ was
  the same error, fixed earlier).
- **Name forms against `data/cast.json`.** Rena calls them とも / ひな / あや and
  was using ともえ / ひなこ / トモエ; Yumi calls her 朋絵 and used トモエ; Aya calls
  her れな and used レナ, and once referred to Hinako as 彼女, which a
  fifteen-year-old does not do about a friend standing there.
- **Western dialect from the Tokyo girls.** Hinako 言われたとる / やらん / 言わん /
  来んの, Aya 気しかしん. Both sheets say 標準語、方言なし.
- **First person against the sheet.** Hinako and Aya are pinned 私 and reached
  for あたし.
- **長距離の奴** for the long-distance man. 遠距離 is the relationship; 長距離 is
  a coach or a race.

Each rewrite keeps the sentence and changes only the word. Idempotent, backs
the save up, prints every change.

    python tools/fix_cast_voice.py [--db data/world.db] [--dry]
