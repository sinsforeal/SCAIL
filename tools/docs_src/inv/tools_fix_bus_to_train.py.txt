# tools\fix_bus_to_train.py  (83 lines)

DOCSTRING:
One-time: she said "on the bus" and there is no bus.

`data/places.json` has four modes -- walk, train, bike, car -- and no bus edge
anywhere, so the trip she meant (the Pixar exhibition in Koto, tomorrow) is
西武池袋線 to Ikebukuro then 有楽町線, about 57 minutes with one change. The
prompt now carries that as fact (`Situation.travel_line` / `trip_lines`), which
stops the next one; this fixes the two rows already on the record, because both
are read back to her -- the message as her own history, the anticipation as an
event of the day.

Three places, because her record and his view must not diverge (that is what
`Store.update_message`'s comment is about: her history and his Telegram came
apart in four places on 2026-08-20 and neither could be put back):

  1. `messages` in data/world.db -- her context
  2. `events` in data/world.db   -- the 08-24 anticipation
  3. the hub's own log           -- what he sees in the app, edited through
                                    /bot/edit so the open app updates live

Idempotent: it matches on "bus" and does nothing once they read "train".
Backs the save up to data/retired/<ts>/ first. The hub must be running for
step 3; if it is not, the first two still happen and it says so.
