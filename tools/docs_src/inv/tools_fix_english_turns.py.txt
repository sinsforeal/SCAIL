DOCSTRING:
One-time: English where Japanese should be, in her own record.

Her world is Japanese. `ja` is what was said and `text` is the operator's
gloss of it, everywhere except his chat. Two things had broken that.

1. A parse artefact. The model split one turn in two -- the Japanese in one,
   the gloss in the next -- and the second read as that person typing English
   in the group chat (Aya, 2026-08-27 20:00: "Tomoe, you take Rena-chan's
   words too seriously"). The gloss belongs to the turn above it, so this
   puts it back. `Exchange._clean` now does the same at write time.

2. The pre-convention era. Every turn from 2026-08-20 to 08-23 is English
   only -- 152 of them -- written before the channel had a language
   underneath. There is no Japanese to restore and inventing one would be
   worse than an honest absence, so they are marked `rendered_only` instead:
   the app renders them as a marked gloss rather than as anybody's words, and
   `Exchange.prompt_text` now reads `ja` first, so they no longer reach her
   own head as her friends speaking English.

Idempotent. Backs the save up to data/retired/<ts>/ first.
