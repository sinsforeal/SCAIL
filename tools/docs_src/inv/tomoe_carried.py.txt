What she is carrying from the other room.

Her chat with him and her chat with her friends are two different rooms, and
each was represented in the other by a keyhole of raw messages: the cast calls
got `recent_messages(4)` cut to 80 characters, laid out exactly like the chat
she was actually in. That is wrong twice over. It is too little -- her friends
ask her about him and she was answering from four lines -- and it is the wrong
KIND of thing, because a transcript in the prompt reads as a conversation to
answer, and the one place she must never be confused is which conversation she
is in.

So neither side gets a transcript of the other. It gets a corpus: where things
stand, what she knows about him with the fade already applied
(`Memory.facts_for_prompt`), what is open between them, what it left her, and
only then the last few things that were actually said -- labelled as a record
and written as the past. Available information that weighs on her, in her own
words the way a memory is, never a live thread with a reply due.

The reverse is the same shape: when she is talking to HIM, the group is a
thing she is carrying -- what it has been about, where she stands in it, who
is still up -- and only the conversation actually in flight is marked as
happening now.

No model call. Everything here is assembled from what the record already holds.
