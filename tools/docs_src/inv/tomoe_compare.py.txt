DOCSTRING:
Social comparison: measuring herself against the people around her.

His ask (2026-08-26): jealousy was entirely about him. Peer comparison --
looks, weight, clothes, being half a step behind Rena, never having had a
boyfriend framed as a social fact (undateable, rumours) rather than a
romantic one -- is core adolescent psychology, is all over her bible, and
had no mechanism.

Code decides WHEN: a peer is in the scene or she has just talked with one,
and the felt probability comes off validation_craving, shame and anxiety
above their own baselines and belonging below par, under a daily cap and a
cooldown. A call (`compare` role) decides WHAT out of the record only: who,
which dimension, which direction, the thought in her own head-voice, and
which self-belief it lands on. `fires: false` is a legal answer and should
be the common one.

Effects are code. Upward (she comes off worse): `envy` toward that person
(a fifth sentiment kind, weeks to fade, per person), shame, craving, a
little anxiety, and a strain on the self-belief the model named
(`plain_underneath`, `alone_is_shameful`, ...) -- which is how "half a step
behind Rena" hardens into "plain underneath" over a term. Downward: a
little pride. Level: nothing but the thought. The thought goes out through
the reflections channel, so her prompt, `cast_reply` for that person and
the director's inner hint all see it, and nothing is ever said aloud.

Readers of `envy`: `Spirals.seedability` (someone she measures herself
against can start a chain), the meters line in `cast_reply`, the sentiment
prompt text and `/sentiment`.
