# tools\fix_dialect.py  (91 lines)

DOCSTRING:
Kansai in Tomoe's mouth, Hakata in Rena's, and a Chinese character.

Audited every line of all six speakers. Three kinds of error, each one the
opposite of what the sheets say:

**Tomoe** is Hakata-born and her bible bans Kansai by name (やん, へん, ねん).
Four lines carry real Kansai: 開いてへん / してへん (Hakata is 開いとらん /
しとらん) and ほんま / ほんまに (Hakata is ほんと / ほんとに).

**Rena-chan** is a Tokyo kid whose sheet says 標準語、方言は使わない, and three of
her lines are western: 生きとる, 〜やけど, 言うてた. One also has her saying
お前ら to her friends, which is a rough masculine plural no fifteen-year-old
girl uses of her own group.

**绫** is the simplified Chinese form. The character does not belong in
Japanese text at all, and the person meant is Aya (亜矢). Same class as the
錢 in Kenji's line.

Each rewrite keeps the sentence and changes the dialect or the character.
Idempotent, backs the save up, prints every change.

    python tools/fix_dialect.py [--db data/world.db] [--dry]
