What she already knows about the six people in her life.

Her memory of other people started empty and stayed nearly empty: 118 facts
about him, and three about her mother, her father and her brother put together
-- one each -- with nothing at all about the three girls she is in the group
chat with every day. The compile was asked for such facts and told "most days
have none", and it obliged nine days running.

A person does not arrive at fifteen knowing nothing about her own family. This
writes the floor: what she would already have, so the compile has something to
add to instead of something to invent from.

**The bound is what she could actually have observed**, and it is different for
the two halves of her world:

- Her family she has had all her life, so the facts go deep -- but only as
  deep as a fifteen-year-old looks. She knows her mother gave up the clinic in
  Kashii for the transfer; she does not know the figure her mother keeps under
  the insurance wall. She knows her father leaves at seven; she does not know
  he is no longer a section chief, because `data/cast.json` says he has not
  said so, and a seed that contradicts the sheet is worse than no seed.
- Her friends she met in **April**, when the transfer put her in a Tokyo high
  school knowing nobody. That is one term and a summer -- five months. So what
  she has of them is logistics and this term's incidents, not history: which
  station, which club, who has a sister, what happened in the first week. And
  it is uneven, the way it is in life: Aya (closeness 74) more than Rena (42),
  Rena more than Hinako (34).

Nothing is here that she has had no occasion to learn. Rena's and Aya's
birthdays are absent because neither has come round since April; Hinako's is
in because the sheet says the party is looming.

`ts` is when she plausibly learned it and `last_seen` is now, which is the
honest shape: a thing you still know is a thing you keep meeting. The fade
(`Memory.score`, half-lives 5/14/35/120/400 days by weight) then leaves the
structural facts standing for years and lets the small ones go.
