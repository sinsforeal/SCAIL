Where her parents are, and whether the car is.

His ask (2026-08-26): dad's car needs dad's schedule, and mom's, well
enough defined that a family trip can actually happen, that there are
times he cannot pick her up, and times he could and does not want to.

Everything here is drawn from `data/household.json` (`schedules` and
`vehicle`) with seeded per-day variation, so the same date gives the same
day on every restart and every reader agrees: the schedule writer (a
family day is a block with the family in it), the director, the beat
writer, the situation's pickup, `/family` and the ops view. No LLM.

**Dad** (Kenji): Mon-Fri out at 07:00, home 21:10-22:30 drawn per day;
Wednesday is no-overtime day on paper and he makes it home by 20:00
about twice a month; a Friday nomikai a few times a month puts him home
past 23:30 and in no state to drive; about one Saturday a month is a
client golf day or a trade show; a two-night trip to the Hakata branch
every couple of months; Sundays and holidays home. **Mom** (Yumi): Tue,
Wed, Fri and alternate Saturdays 09:30-14:30 at the Life; errands; the
Sunday call to Kashii about seven. **The car** is the one thing they did
not sell in the move: dad commutes by train, so on a weekday it sits in
the monthly lot six minutes away, and mom has a licence she does not
like using in Tokyo.

`can_pickup(ts, from_node)` is the honest answer: no car, no; nobody home,
no; dad drunk, no; past his cut-off, no; dad home and tired on a weekday
night, a coin toss he can lose ("take the last train, I'm not getting
dressed"); mom only in daylight and only close. `trip(date)` is a family
day when dad is off, seeded, about one Sunday or holiday in five, with a
destination the car can reach.
