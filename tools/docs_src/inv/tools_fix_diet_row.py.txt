One-time: the media-diet row that is not media.

`experience` fired on a scene of her rereading his message (the trigger caught
the verb "reading" and there was no object test yet), so "his split message
(text): he just stopped there, mid-sentence..." went into `media_diet` -- the
list her system prompt renders as what she is into, and the list the next
experience call is handed as "WHAT SHE HAS BEEN INTO, continue these". Left
there it seeds more of itself. `experience.object_regex` stops new ones;
this drops the one that got through.

From now on `/context del diet <n>` does this from the ops bar: `diet` was
added to the editable sections in sim/context.py, so no script is needed again.

Idempotent, and backs the save up to data/retired/<ts>/ first.
