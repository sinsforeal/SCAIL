Her beliefs, in the language she thinks in.

`beliefs` (what she has decided about him) and `self_beliefs` (about herself)
were written in English and rendered straight into her system prompt: "You are
just a box he checks when he is tired of being alone." That is the sentence she
carries around, so it is hers, so it is Japanese -- the same argument as her
friends and her diary.

Translated, not rewritten: the English is a faithful record of what she
decided, and the point is the same belief in her own words, not a new one. Both
prose fields go over -- `text` (the belief) and `from` (what it grew out of) --
and the `was` history with them, so the old wording a revision kept is still
readable beside the new. Ids, numbers, states and flags are untouched, because
`anchor` matching and `_graduate` key on the id.

    python tools/beliefs_ja.py [--db data/world.db] [--dry]

Idempotent: a field already in Japanese is skipped. Backs the save up first.
