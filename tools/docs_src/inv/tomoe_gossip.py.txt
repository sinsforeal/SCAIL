DOCSTRING:
Something to talk about that is not the four of them.

Measured over 175 group turns: 77% carried a time, a plan or an obligation
and 13% carried a joke, a reaction, an appetite or a gripe. The chat was a
committee because the committee's business was the only thing in the world.
`cast_arcs` gives each of them a storyline, `cast_life` their days and
`social_graph` what they hold about each other -- and every one of those is
about the group itself or about Tomoe.

This is the rest of the world: a boy in the other class, a teacher's
haircut, a couple that broke up over the holidays, a shop that closed, a
girl who got a part-time job she is too young for, a video everyone has
seen. Written by the `gossip` role (`gossip.every_hours`) from what the
group could actually have heard -- the strangers she has already met
(`tomoe/npc.py`), what is going on in Japan this week (`tomoe/world/zeitgeist.py`),
the school calendar and their own arcs -- and kept as a small pool with a
heat that decays.

An item is not a plot: nothing here reaches her life, nobody in it becomes
a friend, and it is over when it cools. It exists to be the thing a message
is ABOUT when the message is not about a meeting time.

kv `gossip`: {"date", "items": [{id, what, who_about, source, heat, born,
last_used, knows: [names]}]}.
