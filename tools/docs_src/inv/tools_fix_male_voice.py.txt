The two men in her house were written in a woman's register.

Their sheets now pin it (`prompts/cast/dad.md`, `kaito.md`), but the record is
what the next call reads, so the lines already stored teach it. Every one of
these was found by reading all forty of their turns, and each rewrite keeps the
sentence and changes only the ending:

Kenji, 46, her father, six lines with a mother's endings -- 〜なよ, 〜ないでね,
〜からね, 〜てあげる, 〜の？ -- which is Yumi's register, not his. Plus 錢, which
is not the Japanese character for money at all.

Kaito, 11, her brother, calling his older sister 君 and あんた. A boy of eleven
says 姉ちゃん. He also writes オレ once where he writes 俺 everywhere else, and
calls his mother ママ where this house says お母さん.

Idempotent, backs the save up, and prints every change it makes.
