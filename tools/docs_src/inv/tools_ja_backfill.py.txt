DOCSTRING:
Put her old record back into Japanese.

Her world is Japanese; the English gloss is not written any more. What is left
is history: every turn from 2026-08-20 to 08-23 was written before the channel
had a language underneath (152 of them, English only), and every `gist` ever
stored is English because the field only just became Japanese. The app renders
both, so her threads still showed English.

Marking them was not enough -- his call. They are TRANSLATED here rather than
dropped: the English is a faithful record of what was said, so the Japanese is
a restoration of the words, not an invention of events.

Register is the same as `tomoe/prompts/cast_turn.txt` describes and is passed to the
call per speaker: the girls are Tokyo JK, standard, no dialect and never
Kansai; mom and dad speak like adults; Kaito is eleven; Tomoe is standard Tokyo
on purpose with Hakata-ben only where she slips.

    python tools/ja_backfill.py [--db path] [--dry] [--limit N]

One call per exchange that needs turns, and the gists in batches. Idempotent:
a turn that already has `ja` and a gist already in Japanese are skipped, so it
can be re-run after an interruption. Backs the save up first.
