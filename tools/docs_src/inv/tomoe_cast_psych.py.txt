DOCSTRING:
What she holds with the people around her -- the part the him-side
machinery never covered.

Rupture and repair (`sim/rupture.py`), expectations (`sim/expectations.py`)
and withholding (`sim/withholding.py`) are keyed to him: a fight with Rena
healed by the clock, "Rena said she'd send the photo" came due nowhere, and
she could not once decide to leave Hinako on read. This module is the
same four mechanics turned toward the cast, plus the one only friendships
have -- confidences: who she has told what, and who therefore does not know.

Code decides when: an exchange finishing is the moment (one `bond` call
reads it), the day boundary ages what is open (an unmended thing scars; a
promise past due is missed), and leaving somebody on read is a roll off
her standing with them. The model decides what: whether that exchange was
a break, a repair, a promise, a thing told in confidence. Numbers move by
clamped steps through code; sentiments (`sim/sentiment.py`) carry what
lasts; memories are written by the moment (`source="bond"`).

kv `cast_bonds`: {roster key: {ruptures: [...], promises: [...],
confidences: [...], left: [...]}}. Readers: her turn prompt and theirs
(`line_for_her`, `line_for_them`), her own prompt (`prompt_text`), the
planner (`brief`), `/bonds`.
