DOCSTRING:
The first character's day: a schoolgirl's timetable in Tokyo, coloured by
a model. Hers alone -- the bells, the commute, the after-school block and the
household at dinner are facts about her -- and the shared pieces it builds on
(the wake, the validation, the contiguity rule) stay in `sim/schedule.py`.

The skeleton is deterministic and depends only on the calendar. The LLM
colors it (which friend, which place, what chore) and may shift times, but
the day always has a spine even if the model call fails.
