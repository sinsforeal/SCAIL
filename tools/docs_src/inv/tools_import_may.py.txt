Bring the May 2026 era (SCAIL v2) into the v3 save file.

Usage: python tools/import_may.py <retired_dir> [--db data/world.db]

The old chat state and diary are not imported verbatim into her prompt: the
old system had no wall between the fiction and the machinery. Direct messages
go into the messages table for the record (they age out of her live prompt by
delivery.history_max_age_hours), and each day is recompiled through the
import_day prompt into a days row she can carry as memory. Facts about him are
extracted from those recompiled days plus the old RAG table and filtered once
more. A short backstory paragraph is stored under kv "backstory" and joins her
system prompt.
