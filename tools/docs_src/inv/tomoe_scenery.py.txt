Borrowed eyes: real photographs, from real Japanese people, sent as hers.

His ask (2026-08-26): "real images. real locations. find twitter accounts
to scrape, find suitable images and start making a database of real
places and things, even if it's not exactly her Nerima or her bench. Find
recent posts, follow accounts that regularly post, and the persona adopts
their life, kind of -- surprise images from real Japanese people somewhat
like her, that I would never have seen." Plus a management layer so the
lore does not get messed up.

**Collecting is stopped** (his call, 2026-09-08): `scenery.harvest` is
false, so stages 1 and 2 below no longer run on their own clock and the
only look session left on X is her own scroll (`tomoe/social.py`). What is
already on file still goes out -- 641 usable photographs against one a day
-- so stages 3 and 4 are unchanged, and `/scenery harvest` still opens a
session by hand.

Four stages, all on her own X account and her own eyes:

**This is machinery, not her.** She "took" these photos; she never
searched for them. So nothing here runs inside her X look, nothing is
written to her feed or her seen list, and no account is followed on her
behalf: the harvester opens its own session on its own clock
(`harvest_every_hours`), when she is not looking, and keeps a watchlist
in kv `scenery_accounts`. Her record shows the photo she sent and
nothing before it.

1. **Discover** (`searches_per_run` quiet searches, `xm.search_posts(
   record=False)`), aimed rather than round-robin: `next_query` takes the
   worst gaps between where she actually stands (`demand`, off her own
   scenes and the next days' plan) and what is on file for that kind and
   hour, and searches `kind_queries` for those. New authors are judged by
   the `scenery_judge` role from their posts' text and one image
   description. The bar is the photographs, not the person: `follow` is
   somebody's own snapshots, `place` is a ward or park or shop feed whose
   pictures are still real pictures of real places, and both go on the
   watchlist. He cannot tell a Saitama platform from a Nerima one; what
   has to be right is the light, the weather and the hour.
2. **Harvest** (each run, `accounts_per_run` of the watchlist, oldest-
   visited first, `xm.fetch_user_posts`): their image posts are
   downloaded to `data/scenery/img/` and described
   by Gemini into a fixed JSON: place kind, indoor/outdoor, time of day,
   season, weather, objects, visible text and names, whether it is a
   selfie or a portrait (people in a street or a park are fine; a person
   as the subject is not, since she is not in it and it is not her
   friend), whether a girl her age could plausibly have taken it from
   where she stands. Rows go to the `scenery` table with the source kept
   (author, post URL, posted time) -- attribution is never dropped.
3. **Pick and send** (`offer`, once a day at most, `send_p`): when her
   scene is a place the database has, in the right season, at the right
   hour, in compatible weather, not a portrait, and with no name that
   contradicts her lore, one image becomes "a photo she just took". The
   impulse is forced with the description as the whole hint, her bubbles
   are the caption, and the photo goes out before them (a `photo` row in
   the hub, an <img> in the app).
4. **Adopt** (the management layer): a sent image's concrete details
   (objects, visible text, the caption) are written to kv `scenery_lore`
   under its place kind, one self-memory, one event. The picker rejects a
   later image whose visible names contradict what has been adopted for
   that place, and for local kinds (her station, her konbini, her park,
   her library) rejects a sign that names a place that is not one of hers
   (`local_names`, drawn wide -- her line, the wards around her). So the
   second photo of "her station" cannot show a different station's sign.

Where she is, for stage 3, is not guessed from the words the director
wrote: the node `embodied/situation.py` walked her to carries a `kind` in
`data/places.json`, a live journey's leg is `train` or `street`, and
`place_map` is only the fallback. `/scenery gaps` is the ruler for stage
1: what she needs, what is on file, and what the next search is for.

Never reposted: `posting.py` writes text only, and nothing here hands an
image to it. The images are other people's; they exist here for one
viewer, with their source, and `/scenery` shows where each came from.
