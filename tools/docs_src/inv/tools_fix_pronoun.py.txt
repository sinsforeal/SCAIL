DOCSTRING:
One line where Tomoe called herself 俺.

    ……てか、パパ。明日の朝、8時45分に出るって、俺も行くの？

俺 is a boy's word. She is fifteen and a girl, and nothing was governing her
first person until `prompts/style_ja.md` named it, so a call reached for the
masculine form in a sentence addressed to her father. It sits in `cast_chat`,
which is read back into her own prompt every call, so it teaches the next one.

Per speaker, because the same word is CORRECT for other people in her life:
Kaito is eleven and says 俺 four times, Kenji is her father and says it once,
and both are right. Only Tomoe's own first person is wrong -- and not even all
of hers: 「『手洗ってこないと唐揚げは俺が食べる』ってことだ」is her quoting
Kaito inside 「」, which is how anybody reports what a boy said.

Idempotent. Backs the save up first.

    python tools/fix_pronoun.py [--db data/world.db] [--dry]
