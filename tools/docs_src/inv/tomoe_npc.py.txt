DOCSTRING:
The other people -- the ones she runs into rather than the ones she has.

The cast is seven people with sheets, sentiments, a social graph and a place
in her prompts. Everybody ELSE in Tokyo did not exist: she rode a packed train
with `with: "strangers"` in her schedule and nobody on it, stood in a konbini
with nobody behind the till, and spent a school day in a building holding one
class of girls none of whom were ever in the room. A world where only the seven
people who matter exist is not a world; it is a stage set.

**Who is plausibly there comes from the place, not from a list of characters.**
`data/places.json` already gives every node a `kind` -- konbini, station,
school, library, park, shrine, museum, shotengai, city -- and that is the
spine: a konbini has somebody behind the till and somebody else buying milk, a
platform has a school kid from another school and a woman who is too close, a
shrine has an old woman. Nobody is in her flat who is not family, because her
family IS the cast.

**Three things a person can be**, which is how it works in life:

- a face she has never seen and will never see again -- the man who takes the
  seat she was walking towards,
- a face she knows by sight and has no name for -- the clerk on the evening
  shift, the boy always on the 7:52. Code reuses a face already seen AT THAT
  PLACE with probability `known_face_p`, which is why the konbini has a regular
  clerk rather than a new stranger every visit,
- a face with a name, which she only gets where you would actually learn one:
  school, or a face met `name_after` times.

**Code decides when and who; the model decides what.** The gate is the place,
a gap, a daily cap and a roll (`_should`); the slot -- known face or new, and
which role from the place's own table -- is picked in code and handed over; the
call writes who that person concretely is and what passed between them. Most
encounters have no words in them at all.

**They stay out of the cast machinery.** An NPC never enters `relationships`,
`sentiments`, the social graph, `cast_chat` or the group; there is no standing,
no closeness, no beat. That is deliberate: the empty-person fault of 2026-08-27
came from a name reaching a lookup that had no row for it. What an encounter
DOES leave is an `events` row, which is the same door everything else in her
day comes through -- the timeline, the diary, her memories and the director all
read events already, so a stranger reaches her the way the weather does.

A face she keeps meeting is a candidate for the cast, and this never writes
`data/cast.json`: promotion is his, and `/npc` names the candidates.

An encounter reaches her through `events` and nothing else, which is enough:
the director reads the day's events (so the scene answers a stranger), the
diary reads them all, and her own voice to him reads only `salience >= 2` --
so the person on the till never reaches her chat with him and the man who
said something that stayed with her does.

Store: kv `npcs` (the faces), `npc_moments` (what happened), `npc_counts`,
`npc_last_ts`, `npc_last_check_ts`. Ops: `/npc`.
