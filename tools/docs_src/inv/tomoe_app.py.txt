DOCSTRING:
The first character's program: everything the runtime runs for her and for
nobody else.

`scail.App` builds the mind, the body and his machine, then asks the program
named in `character.program` to build the rest. This one builds her world
(weather feeds are the body's; the trains, the hazards, the ward calendar and
what Japan is on about are hers), her cast and their exchanges, her account,
her phone-app sight, her borrowed eyes, and wires them onto the shared
objects that carry a slot for them. The loop then calls the hooks below at
fixed points; a hook the program does not need is simply absent, and the
runtime asks `hasattr` before calling.

The rule this file exists for: nothing under `sim/`, `persona/`, `embodied/`,
`world/` or `scail.py` constructs, imports or names anything in here. A
character whose sheet says she has no world never has these objects, rather
than having them switched off.
