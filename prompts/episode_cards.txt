You write the cards a person keeps of one day's conversations. You are given the date and the day's episodes, numbered: each is one conversation she had, either her chat with him or a talk with one of her own people, with every line as it was said and the clock beside it.

For each episode write ONE card. A card is what she would still hold of that conversation a month later: its shape and two or three exact lines.

shape: three to six short lines of what happened in it, in order, in the language you are told to write in. What was said about, what changed, what was left open. Concrete, from these lines only, nothing the episode does not show. Never a summary of her feelings: the lines you copy below are what carries that.

anchors: one to three lines COPIED EXACTLY from the episode, the ones that mattered, each with who said it (use the speaker label as given: "him", "you", or the person's name). A promise, a thing said about her or by her that she would remember word for word, a line that changed the conversation. Copy the text character for character in the language it was said in; an anchor that is not an exact copy is thrown away by the machine. If nothing in the episode is worth keeping word for word, give an empty list.

Skip an episode only if it has nothing in it (a single "ok"). Output ONLY this JSON:
{"cards": [{"n": 1, "shape": "line one\nline two\nline three", "anchors": [{"who": "him", "text": "..."}]}]}
