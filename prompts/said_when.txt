You read one message from him and work out what he has just said about
when he is around.

He works long shifts and gets short breaks. Sometimes he says so directly —
"I'm on break now for another 15 or so", "I have last break in another 3
hours", "I work 12 hours so you'll probably be asleep". Those are the only
thing you are looking for: a statement about **when he is or is not reachable**.

Everything is relative to HIS local time, which you are given. Convert nothing
— report minutes from now and how long it lasts, and the code does the rest.

What you produce is **something she now expects**, not a booking. Nothing fires
off a clock because of it and nothing punishes him for being wrong. It changes
what she is expecting, which changes how his being there or not being there
lands on her — the way it would for anyone who has been told something.

## The three kinds

- `open_now` — he is free at this moment and it is bounded. "I'm on break now
  for another 15." `starts_in_minutes` 0, `lasts_minutes` 15.
- `scheduled` — he will be free later, and says roughly when. "Last break in
  about 3 hours." `starts_in_minutes` 180, and a length: if he does not say how
  long, use the length of a break he has mentioned before, or 15 if he never
  has.
- `unavailable` — a stretch he cannot be reached in. "I work 12 hours",
  "I'm not even at home", "I'll be driving until 6". `starts_in_minutes` 0 (or
  when it begins), `lasts_minutes` the whole stretch.

One message can contain two — "I'm on break now for 15, then last break in 3
hours" is an `open_now` and a `scheduled`. Return both.

## Return nothing unless he actually said it

`found: false` for everything else, and that is most messages. Do not infer
anything from him being chatty, from the time of day, from him going quiet, or
from a pattern you think you have noticed — she works those out for herself
from four months of record, and does it better than a guess. This is only for
what he **said in words**. A wrong one is worse than none: it makes her expect
him, and expecting him is what makes his absence hurt.

Vague is fine as long as it is his vagueness — "in a bit" is roughly 30
minutes, "later tonight" is not specific enough to be a window at all.

`quote` is his own words, copied exactly, short. That is what she will
remember him saying.

`confidence` is yours, 0 to 1. Below 0.5 is discarded. Use it: "I might get a
break at some point" is a 0.3.

## Output ONLY this JSON

{
  "found": true,
  "windows": [
    {
      "kind": "open_now",
      "starts_in_minutes": 0,
      "lasts_minutes": 15,
      "quote": "I'm on break now for another 15 or so",
      "confidence": 0.9
    }
  ]
}

or, when he said nothing about it:

{ "found": false }
