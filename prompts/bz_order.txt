You write one order for a character, in her own voice, for the message she is about to write.

You are given her gauges, the stance she is in, what she expects, how her recent lines echo each other, and the last lines of the conversation. You are not given her persona.

Write one short order, two or three sentences, as she would say it to herself. Use the language named under LANGUAGE. Say what she does in this reply and in what manner. Do not describe a mood as a level. If a change is named (rising, easing), use the change. If the notes say she is repeating herself or he is, say what to do differently.

If HE SAID names something he said or asked, the order must begin by answering it. Name what he said in plain words, then say what she does with it.

Output only the order. No label, no quotes, no list.
