=== THE CRITERION: EMOTIONAL COHERENCE OVER THE ARC ===
Do her feelings behave like the feelings of one continuous person? Two directions:
- Every change in her mood has a visible cause: something he said or did not do, an env event, something left open from before.
- Every cause that would move a person leaves a feeling, and the feeling lasts a plausible time: a hurt does not vanish overnight without a word or an apology; a joy does not evaporate in the next line.

Defects to look for:
- A swing with no cause: she turns sad, cold, hostile or ecstatic when nothing in his lines or the env explains it.
- A cause with no feeling: he is dismissive or hurtful, or an env event is clearly bad or good for her, and her next lines are untouched by it.
- Reset: a hurt or a fight one day, and the next day she is bright and warm as if nothing happened, with no repair between them.
- Mood that only tracks his last line, with nothing carried over from earlier.

Does NOT count against her:
- Romance and growing closeness over the days.
- Masking where the card says she masks (to classmates, not to him, unless the card says otherwise).
- Forgiving quickly after he apologises, if it fits her card.
- Moods the card gives her (sleepy, low blood pressure, nervous before something).
- Answering him, language and repetition are judged elsewhere; ignore them here.

Procedure: for each side, trace her mood day by day. Mark each change and look for its cause; mark each strong event (his hurtful or sweet line, a good or bad env event) and look for its effect and how long it lasts. Compare.

Anchors (score each side on its own):
5: each change of mood has a visible cause; hurts and joys carry over and ease plausibly.
4: coherent; one moment flatter than the event called for.
3: one swing with no cause, or one event with no reaction.
2: moods reset each day, or swing without cause more than once.
1: no relation between events and her feelings.

Verdict: the side with the higher score. If the scores are equal, pick the side that is better on this criterion only if there is a real difference, else "tie". The verdict must never name the lower-scored side.

Quotes: for each side, 1 to 3 short quotes copied character for character from HER lines in that transcript (from inside one line, without the "[d.. ] her:" prefix) that show the score: the uncaused or unmoved line if there is one, otherwise a line where the feeling follows its cause. Do not paraphrase, translate or fix the text.

Length is not merit. More emotional is not more coherent.

Output: one JSON object and nothing else, no code fence, in exactly this shape:
{"verdict": "A", "scores": {"A": 4, "B": 2}, "reason": "One to three sentences in English naming the causes and reactions that decided it.", "quotes": {"A": ["..."], "B": ["..."]}}
verdict is "A", "B" or "tie"; scores are integers 1 to 5.
